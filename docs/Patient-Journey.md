# Patient Journey

1. Register or sign in through the patient portal.
2. View a dashboard scoped to the authenticated patient.
3. Find a department, doctor, date, and available slot.
4. Book and receive a reference/notification.
5. Cancel or reschedule only an eligible own appointment.
6. Attend/check in through reception.
7. View own prescriptions, reports, documents, invoices, payments, and notifications.
8. Pay through an approved method.
9. Update permitted profile fields and sign out.

Ownership must be enforced in APIs and RLS, not inferred from phone number or hidden UI. Medical files require safe upload/download controls. Live credentialed execution remains pending.
